— Our purpose

More than news.
A movement.

PixelHive Media is a developing digital-media platform built around Kenyan stories, global perspective, creative production and community conversation.

We aim to make information useful, engaging and accessible to a new generation. Our standard is simple: verify before publishing, distinguish fact from opinion, correct mistakes transparently, and treat people fairly — including when covering celebrity news and gossip.
