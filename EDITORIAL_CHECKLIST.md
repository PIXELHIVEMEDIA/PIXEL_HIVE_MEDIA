## Review every story

- [ ] Open and read the original source.
- [ ] Confirm the story is current and the date is correct.
- [ ] Verify important facts against reliable sources.
- [ ] Check names, figures, locations and quotations.
- [ ] Seek a second reliable source for significant or disputed claims.
- [ ] Write an original PixelHive summary.
- [ ] Attribute information and link to sources.
- [ ] Avoid copying another publisher's full article or protected images.
- [ ] Check for misleading headlines, privacy risks and unnecessary harm.
- [ ] Remove duplicate or outdated stories.

## Final approval

- [ ] Every included story has been checked.
- [ ] Headlines accurately represent the facts.
- [ ] Links work and attribution is present.
- [ ] The edition has been proofread.
- [ ] An editor has explicitly approved publication.

## Publishing rule

Do not publish automatically collected leads as verified news.
Only publish stories that have passed the checks above and received
explicit editorial approval.

If verification is incomplete, hold the story or clearly label
the uncertainty.
